मराठीत थोडक्यात

हे ॲप तुमची कोणतीही वैयक्तिक माहिती गोळा करत नाही किंवा कोणालाही पाठवत नाही. खाते नाही, जाहिराती नाहीत, analytics नाही. तुमची प्रगती फक्त तुमच्या फोनवर राहते. रेकॉर्डिंग तात्पुरते असते आणि फोनबाहेर जात नाही. "शब्द ओळख" फोनवरच चालते.

What the app stores

Your learning progress (lessons finished, answers, streak, settings) is stored only on your device. It isn't sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Microphone and recordings

The microphone is used only when you choose to practise speaking. Recordings are temporary files on your device, deleted when you leave the screen, and never uploaded. The optional word check uses your phone's built-in on-device speech recogniser in offline mode, so your voice stays on your phone. The app works without microphone permission.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules local notifications on your device. No notification data is sent to a server.

Text-to-speech

English sentences are read aloud by your phone's own text-to-speech voice.

Backups

You can export your progress as text and share it wherever you choose. The app sends it nowhere by itself.

What we do not do

No account or login, no advertising, no advertising ID, no analytics or tracking, no location, no contacts, no sale or sharing of data.

Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
