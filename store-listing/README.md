**English (en-IN)**
```
English Boluya — learn in your language, speak in English!

A spoken-English app made for Marathi speakers. Every idea is explained in Marathi first; then you hear the English sentence, say it and use it.

✔ From the very beginning
From letters and sounds to everyday conversation. Four paths: Foundation, Beginner, Intermediate and Professional (preview).

✔ Short daily steps
Each lesson has 7 short stages: goal and conversation, meaning and formula, listen and repeat, practice, your own speaking, role-play, review. You always see how many minutes are left.

✔ Lots of practice
119 lessons, 800+ questions, 119 scripted role-plays, 43 everyday phrases and 100 everyday words. Missed questions come back for review at the right time.

✔ Speaking practice
Hear each sentence in the phone's voice (also slowly), say it aloud, and record yourself to compare if you like. On supported phones, an on-device "word check" shows which words of your sentence the phone recognised (e.g. "2 of 3 words heard"). It's word recognition only, not a pronunciation score or a test.

✔ Grammar in simple Marathi
12 tenses, modal verbs, passive voice and If / reported speech in one chart, each with a summary and examples.

✔ Encouragement, not pressure
Today's progress, your streak, a weekly summary and small celebrations when you finish a stage. Took a break? No problem — your progress is kept.

✔ Offline, no account
All lessons work without internet. No login, no ads. Your progress stays on your phone; you can export a backup yourself.

Also: daily reminder, dark mode, larger text.

Note: the app does not certify language level or promise fluency in a fixed time. A little practice every day is what works.
```

## 4. Data safety

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Does the app collect or share any of the required user data types? | **No** |
| Why "No" is true | No account, analytics, ads or crash reporting. Progress is stored only on the phone. Recordings are temporary files on the phone, deleted on leaving the screen, never uploaded. The word check uses the phone's on-device recogniser (offline mode is forced); audio never leaves the phone. Daily reminders are local notifications. Backup is a text the user shares themselves. |
| Is data encrypted in transit? / deletion request | Not asked when nothing is collected |

Re-check these answers if you ever add analytics, crash reporting, login or a cloud speech service.

**Permissions the release build declares and why (if Play asks):**
- `RECORD_AUDIO`: optional speaking practice: record-and-replay and the on-device word check. Asked once on first launch and when recording; the app works without it.
- `POST_NOTIFICATIONS`, `RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED`, `VIBRATE`, `WAKE_LOCK`: optional daily reminder (local notifications, kept after a restart).
- `INTERNET`, `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`: declared by Expo/Android libraries. The app works fully offline and sends no user data.
- No foreground service, no exact alarms, no location, no contacts, no storage access, no advertising ID.

### Testing tracks (important for a new personal developer account)
- **Personal accounts created after 13 Nov 2023 must run a closed test with at least 12 testers, opted in for 14 continuous days, before Production can be requested.** Organisation accounts are exempt. Your customers can be those testers.
- Order: **Internal testing** (you, instant) → **Closed testing** (customers, by email list or Google Group; they install from Play via the opt-in link) → apply for Production → **staged rollout** (e.g. 20% → 100%).
- Testing through Play is better than sharing an APK file: testers get updates automatically, and the version they install is the same, identically signed one later published, so their progress carries over.

### About sharing an APK file directly
An APK you send is signed with your upload key. Once Play App Signing is on, the Play Store version is signed with Google's key. A tester who installed the shared APK must **uninstall it (losing progress) before installing from Play**. Use direct APKs only for quick one-off previews; use the closed-testing link for customers.
